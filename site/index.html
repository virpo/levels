    How it works

    Your level is how many of the 100 you do; the order decides your tier. Your agent reads check.md, runs a read-only probe that prints names and counts, judges each level, and opens this page with the result in the link after the #. The page saves the 100 answers anonymously, so your card has a link and the stats count it; what your agent noticed stays in your link, and a name or photo goes up only when you share. What it can't see from your machine, you tick yourself, and your card says so.

    * The pros: 94 engineers who show how they work with agents, in posts, talks, repos and config. 69 of the levels link to one of them saying it in their own words. Where good engineers disagree, both sides are there.

    Who made this

    Peter Hraska (@virpo). I spend an unhealthy amount of time with agents and talk to a lot of people about how they use them. "I use AI a lot" turned out to mean very different things, so I wrote down the tricks that are actually worth your time.

    The whole list is open as Markdown and JSON. Wrong check, missing trick, one that should go extinct? Open an issue on GitHub or tellTell me on X.
